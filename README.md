# Kassu Gebresellasie, Ph.D.

AI engineer building agentic workflows. Physicist by training, with a Ph.D. in Computational Data Science and Engineering (North Carolina A&T, 2017), an M.Sc. in physics (Memorial University of Newfoundland) and a visiting-scholar year at MIT's Laboratory for Atomistic and Molecular Mechanics. Founder of Orthoai. Based in the Greater Toronto Area.

## What I work on

- **Multi-agent LLM systems**: agent crews that parse documents, retrieve context, call tools and produce reports. CrewAI, LangChain, LangGraph, FAISS, FastAPI.
- **Evaluation**: designing and grading expert-level scientific tasks for frontier AI models, with graders that are hard to game.
- **Quantum computing**: Pasqal neutral-atom compilation, Quantum Intermediate Representation (pyqir, qadence), quantum machine learning.
- **ML for materials**: predicting mechanical integrity of biodegradable magnesium implants from corrosion, micro-CT and finite-element data.
